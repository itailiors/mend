# Instrument samples

Per-note MP3s from the **Fluid (R3) General MIDI SoundFont** by Frank Wen, as pre-rendered by
[gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts](https://github.com/gleitz/midi-js-soundfonts) (`FluidR3_GM/`), copied from Reharm.

| Folder | GM instrument | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `rhodes/` | electric_piano_1 | C, Eb, Gb, A from C3 to A5 |

Unchanged from the source files. Tone's Sampler repitches the notes in between.
